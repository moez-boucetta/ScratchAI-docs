Scratch AI is a free, open-source Chrome extension that brings an AI chat assistant directly into the Scratch editor at [scratch.mit.edu](https://scratch.mit.edu). Whether you're a curious kid building your first game, an educator scaffolding a classroom project, or a hobbyist who wants to prototype animations fast, Scratch AI lets you describe what you want in plain language and watches it come to life — no manual block-dragging required.

## What is Scratch AI?

Scratch AI adds a dedicated chat panel inside the Scratch editor. Instead of hunting for the right blocks and snapping them together manually, you simply type what you want — "add a cat that chases the mouse" or "create a platformer with a score counter" — and Scratch AI translates your words into real actions on your live project. Every change is immediate and interactive, right inside your browser.

The extension is completely free, fully open-source under the MIT license, and never sends your project data anywhere without your explicit API call to the AI provider you choose.

## The Toolbar Popup and the Editor Panel

Scratch AI has two visible surfaces:

- **Browser toolbar popup** — After installation, click the Scratch AI robot icon in your Chrome toolbar (the puzzle-piece Extensions area). A small popup opens showing the Scratch AI logo, the current version number (v1.0.1), a link to the GitHub repository, and a link to the Scratch AI website. This popup is purely informational; you don't need it to build projects.
- **In-editor chat panel (Scratch AI tab)** — This is where all the action happens. When you open a project at `scratch.mit.edu/projects/…`, a **Scratch AI** tab appears in the editor toolbar next to the standard Code, Costumes, and Sounds tabs. Click it to open the chat panel and start prompting.

## Supported AI Providers

Scratch AI works with your existing API key from any of the following providers:

| Provider | Notable Models |
|---|---|
| **OpenAI** | GPT-4o, GPT-4o mini, o3-mini |
| **Anthropic** | Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Claude 3 Haiku |
| **Google** | Gemini 1.5 Pro, Gemini 1.5 Flash |
| **xAI** | Grok-2, Grok-2 mini |
| **DeepSeek** | DeepSeek-V3, DeepSeek-R1 |
| **Alibaba (Qwen)** | Qwen2.5, Qwen-Turbo |
| **Z.AI** | GLM-4 series |
| **Moonshot** | Moonshot-v1 |
| **Meta Llama** | Llama 3.3 70B (via OpenRouter) |
| **OpenRouter** | Any model in the OpenRouter catalogue |

You store your API key locally in your browser — it is never sent to any Scratch AI server.

## How It Works

When you open a Scratch project in the editor, Scratch AI activates and adds a **Scratch AI** tab to the toolbar. Here is what happens each time you send a prompt:

1. You type a natural-language instruction in the chat input.
2. Scratch AI sends your prompt — along with relevant context about your current project — to the AI provider you configured.
3. The model produces a plan describing which actions to take (add a sprite, write a script, draw a costume, set a variable, and so on).
4. The extension silently carries out those actions against your live project in the browser.
5. Your project updates in real time, right in front of you.

You stay in control at every step: you can keep chatting to refine the result, undo individual actions, or take over the blocks manually at any time.

<Note>
  Scratch AI is released under the **MIT license** and is completely free to use. Your API key is stored only in your local browser storage and is never transmitted to any Scratch AI server — it goes directly from your browser to the AI provider you choose.
</Note>
