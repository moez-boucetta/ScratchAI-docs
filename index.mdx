## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the extension">
    Download the latest build from GitHub and load it as an unpacked Chrome extension. Takes about two minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a Scratch project">
    Navigate to any project on `scratch.mit.edu/projects/…`. The **Scratch AI** tab appears in the editor toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your API key">
    Click the ⚙️ gear icon, pick an AI model, and paste your API key. Scratch AI works with OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, and many more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start building">
    Type what you want — "add a player sprite that moves with arrow keys" — hit **Build**, and Scratch AI edits your project live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Scratch AI is free and open-source under the MIT license. Your API key is stored only in your browser and is never sent anywhere other than the AI provider you choose.
</Note>
